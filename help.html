Frequently asked questions

Browse the most common questions or search for a specific topic. If you can't find what you need, contact our team below.

Already have a booking?

Keep your booking reference nearby when contacting support. It helps us locate your reservation.

How do I book a graduation photoshoot?

Visit the photography packages section, select your preferred package and click Reserve My Package. Complete the reservation form and keep your booking reference for future enquiries.

Where can I find my booking reference?

Your reference is displayed after a successful reservation and is included in your booking email if delivery succeeds. You can also view linked bookings in your client portal.

How does the 50% deposit work?

A 50% deposit is required to secure your session. Your booking details show the amount and banking instructions. Your booking is only confirmed once the studio verifies the deposit.

Where do I send my proof of payment?

Send your payment receipt through WhatsApp support and include your booking reference so our team can match the payment to your reservation.

I haven't received my verification email. What should I do?

Check your Spam or Junk folder and confirm that you registered with the correct email address. If you're still unable to verify your account, contact support for assistance.

How can I track my booking?

Sign in to the client portal to review your booking and payment status. If you booked before registering, use the option to link an existing booking.

Can I change my booking details?

Contact the studio with your booking reference and the details you'd like to change. Our team will confirm whether the requested adjustment is possible.

Which packages include a photobook?

The Signature and Prestige packages include a photobook. The Essential package does not. Visit our packages for the current pricing and full details.
