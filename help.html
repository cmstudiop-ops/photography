Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your graduation photoshoot.

How do I reserve a photography package?

Visit our main website, select your preferred package and click “Reserve My Package”. Complete the booking form and save the reference number shown after submission.

How much is the booking deposit?

A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking. The booking confirmation screen and email provide the amount, bank details and your payment reference. Your reservation is confirmed after the studio verifies your payment.

Where do I send proof of payment?

Send your payment receipt to our WhatsApp support number, +27 81 840 1398, and include your booking reference.

How do I access my booking online?

Visit the Client Portal, sign in with your account and view your booking status. If you booked before creating an account, use the option to link your existing booking.

I haven't received my account verification email. What should I do?

Check your Spam or Junk folder and confirm that you entered the correct email address. If you still cannot access your account, contact us on WhatsApp for assistance.

Can I change my package or booking details?

Contact our support team on WhatsApp with your booking reference. We'll review your request and let you know what changes are possible.
